ATZMA Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

ATZMA is designed to keep each member’s broker connection and workspace isolated from every other member.

1. Data We Store

  • Account profile details such as email and display name.
  • User preferences and control settings.
  • Broker connection metadata and masked credential references.
  • Execution requests, decision summaries, audit events, and system health records.

2. Broker Credentials

Broker API credentials are intended to remain server-side. They are stored in encrypted form and are not returned to the frontend in plaintext.

3. How Data Is Used

  • To authenticate the user and protect access to private workspace data.
  • To verify broker connectivity and support isolated execution requests.
  • To provide auditability, troubleshooting, and operational safety controls.

4. Third-Party Services

ATZMA relies on third-party providers such as Supabase, Alpaca, GitHub Pages, and optional notification providers. Those services may process operational metadata necessary to deliver the platform.

5. Security Posture

ATZMA uses authenticated access controls, encrypted broker credential storage, and server-side execution boundaries to reduce exposure. No online system can guarantee perfect security.

6. Data Retention

Operational records such as execution logs, audit trails, and health events may be retained for safety, incident review, compliance preparation, and service improvement.

7. User Control

Users may disconnect a broker, stop using the service, or request account deletion subject to applicable technical and legal retention needs.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy handling should be directed through the operator’s support channel before enabling live trading.
